## From content to knowledge

A team can write a project operations manual in Spaces, keeping its pages, attachments and revision history together. When full-text retrieval is needed, a project administrator enables the built-in project knowledge base. Wait for indexing, then ask an Assistant to retrieve and cite the material.

Knowledge bases can also ingest external material independently. Spaces can be used for writing and collaboration before indexing is enabled. A saved page and a ready search index are separate states; recent edits may not appear in search immediately. See [Search and reuse](../spaces/search-and-reuse).

## Content, projects and access

Spaces uses project membership, roles, files and Assistant connections. Knowledge bases and external document sources retain their own access checks. Grouping these guides under Knowledge does not share resources or extend permissions.

For planning work, tracking execution and accepting deliverables, see [Projects and Tasks](../../projects/index). Developer APIs and editor extensions are covered in [Content and Editor Runtime](../../plugin/plugin-sdk/content-runtime).
