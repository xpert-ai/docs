This section collects product documentation for Xpert's official applications. Each app has a dedicated product group covering its business purpose, first use, daily operations and results.

To see apps available in your environment, open **Discover and add → Apps** in the current organization. Documentation coverage and installed applications are managed separately.

## Documented applications

| App | Intended work |
| --- | --- |
| [Bid Studio](./bid-studio/index) | Technical, commercial and combined bids for engineering, goods and services, with company evidence, figures and similarity checks |
| [Code Review](./code-review/index) | GitHub PR diffs, AI review, confirmed submission and repository automation |
| [Maps](./maps/index) | Place search, map exploration, walking and driving routes, and place references in chat |
| [Sites](./sites/index) | Conversational static site creation, version previews and management, and publication with controlled access |

Each app has one product article covering core capabilities, the workflow and scope. Open it from the table or its dedicated sidebar group.

## What to look for in an app guide

A product introduction explains the intended users, business problem, inputs and outputs. The quickstart walks through a first real task. Operating guides explain stage states, human confirmation points, recovery after failure and how to save and revisit results.

Workflows vary by application. Use the app's own documentation for screenshots, input examples, project types, task states and export options. Steps requiring an administrator are described in its prerequisites.
