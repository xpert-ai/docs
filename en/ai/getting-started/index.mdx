Xpert AI connects models, tools and business data to an Assistant's workflow. Describe your goal in chat, let the Assistant query authorized data or process files, and save reusable results as Artifacts.

## Collaborate and deliver through projects

Keep ongoing work in a project with shared members, Assistants, instructions and files. Break goals into tasks, track dependencies, background execution and outputs, then accept the actual results.

<Card title="Projects & Tasks" icon="list-check" href="/en/ai/projects/index">
  Organize Assistant assignments, inspect task trees, Gantt and execution history, and manage acceptance and rework.
</Card>

Start with [Complete your first project task](/en/ai/projects/quickstart), or use [Group collaboration](/en/ai/projects/tasks/group-collaboration) when several Assistants need to work together.

## Turn a business question into a live dashboard

For metrics you need to revisit, ask the Assistant to create a live dashboard. It checks the data and metric definitions before generating a page. Continue editing in chat, filter and refresh in Workbench, then confirm publication for read-only team viewing.

<Card title="Live Dashboard" icon="chart-column" href="/en/ai/live-dashboard/index">
  Generate standard charts or HTML/ECharts pages from natural language. Keep refining one dashboard draft, confirm publication and share formal versions.
</Card>

For example: “Show quarterly net sales by month and region, with the total at the top, a trend on the left, details on the right and a region filter.” Start with [Create your first dashboard](/en/ai/live-dashboard/create) to learn which data and business rules to provide.
