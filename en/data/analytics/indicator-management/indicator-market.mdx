The current metric entry point is a workspace list. It has no separate metric market, subscription flow, or market request button from the old product.

## List actions

- Search matches the metric name, code, and description.
- The status selector filters **Draft**, **Published**, and **Archived**.
- The table shows metric type, bound model, publishing state, and certification state.
- Row actions can include edit, delete, certification, publish, business-domain assignment, and query, depending on permission and current state.

## Recommended use

Search by code, confirm the model version and certification state, then click **Query** to verify the result. To give a group of users access automatically, assign the metric to a business domain and let its members inherit access.

If an old page mentions a metric market, subscription request, or project metric application, use this workspace list instead.
